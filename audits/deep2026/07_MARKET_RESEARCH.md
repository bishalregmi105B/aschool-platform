## Nepal market

**Vendors.** Veda/inGrails = leader (1,300+ schools claimed, 99% renewal, **no published pricing — pricing page 404s**, **no AI claims anywhere on its marketing site**, student/parent app 3.8–3.9★ with top-voted reviews reporting hard login failure, iOS 2.8★; Play data-safety label says data **not encrypted and cannot be deleted**). Gurukul = only fully transparent vendor (Free ≤50 students; NPR 900/mo or 8,500/yr; NPR 2,500/mo or 22,000/yr; AI metered 20 lifetime → 500/mo, credits NPR 300/50; BS calendar explicit; eSewa+Khalti+Fonepay). Vidyapith NPR 599/1,299/1,699/mo. Fossa claims "True BS Calendar — not an afterthought". DP-LMS from NPR 2,500/mo. **PDP Shikshya** = the only genuinely AI-first Nepali design: Socratic tutor that refuses answers, RAG grounded on real Nepal Grade 9–10 textbooks with citations + exam-mode deflection, on-device PII stripping, "Proof of Thought" process dashboard, "Analog Handshake" vision check of handwritten work. mySecondTeacher: content, NPR 2,000–2,500/subject/yr.

**Price anchors.** Nepali SaaS floor NPR 599–2,500/month ≈ NPR 7,000–30,000/yr. Per-student: ~NPR 3–5/student/month vs global Capterra benchmark $5.94 avg — **Nepal willingness-to-pay is ~1–2% of the global per-student rate. Per-student pricing imported from US/India will not clear.** Fedena landed at ₹50,000+/yr with one documented Nepal school. Bulk SMS NPR 0.50–2.00/msg. Wallet MDR anecdotal 4–5% (eSewa), Khalti Rs 5–10/txn. Market: 35,951 schools (25,623 community / 8,941 private), 7.04M students; private share growing (19%→25% of schools in 5 yrs). Serviceable private-school SaaS TAM ≈ **NPR 130M/yr — small; the money is in payments, content, and the community-school/government channel.**

**Connectivity.** 56.0% internet penetration; 32.4M mobile connections (109% of population); **85.1% of households have a smartphone, 82% internet access** (NSO). 4G at 89.64% of subscribers. Load-shedding has ENDED — design for *unscheduled* outages and intermittent data, not a schedule. Provincial spread is wide (Bagmati 80% vs Karnali 58.4% smartphone).

**Messaging substrate: Facebook/Messenger, NOT WhatsApp.** FB ad reach 50.0% of population / 89.6% of internet users; Messenger 37.1%; WhatsApp isn't even reported. Viber jumped to #1 on Play during the 2025 platform ban. **An India-style WhatsApp-first playbook is a channel error in Nepal.**

## Nepal regulatory facts (moat material)

- **IEMIS/CEHRD**: Flash I/II twice per session; deadlines with 10-day lead times; **CEHRD's portal is closed to third-party integration so the Excel workbook IS the product**. Recurring errors: unreconciled transfers, prior-year carry-forward, ghost students, "Dalit" spelled three ways. There is a Facebook group named "IEMIS Problems & Solution Nepal". Portals: emis.cehrd.gov.np, ess.cehrd.gov.np.
- **NEB/SEE**: scale verified (A+ 90–100=4.0 … D 35–39=1.6, NG<35=0.0; GPA = Σ(gp×credit)/Σcredit; pass = D in every subject; **Class-12-only rule: <35% in theory alone → NG regardless of practical**; grade-increment re-sits replace the original grade). SEE 2082 internal-assessment notice enumerates **139 subjects** with THFM/THPM/PRFM/PRPM columns — 75/27 theory + 25/10 internal for most; 50/18+50/20 for e.g. Computer Science (3031), Animal Health (4011); entries are **irreversible once uploaded** to OCE.
- **Fee regulation (2072 directive)**: exactly **14 permitted headings**; max 12 months tuition/yr; admission fee once only ≤ 1 month's tuition; annual fee ≤ 2 months' tuition; exam fee ≤ 50% of monthly; materials 10% of monthly once yearly; schedules require local-government approval. First violation ≤ Rs 25,000; repeat can cost the licence. 2026: MoEST press release + **Supreme Court interim order (2 Apr 2026)** + directive to all 753 local governments + help desk. Private schools collect ~Rs 15B/yr in fees.
- **Data protection**: no comprehensive act; Privacy Act 2075 is the principal statute (≤3 yrs + NPR 30,000). Draft PDP Policy 2082 (July 2025) + IT & Cybersecurity Bill 2082 (Clause 61: destroy data within 35 days of purpose fulfilment) are coming. **Building to India-DPDP-grade minor-consent now is cheap insurance.**
- **National AI Policy 2082** (approved Aug 2025): AI subjects in curricula, 5,000 AI professionals — permission, not constraint; "aligns with National AI Policy 2082" is available positioning.
- **Academic session 2083**: classes from Baisakh 21; **a two-day Saturday–Sunday weekend for schools — forcing every school to rebuild its timetable into a five-day week.** Kathmandu Metro issues its own calendar → local-level calendar variance must be data, not code.
- **Biometric analysis** (Nepal-specific): fingerprint hardware realistically NPR 40,000–90,000 for three points; accuracy degrades with humidity/dry skin, disproportionately failing children from manual-labour families; RFID doesn't prove who tapped (~75% buddy-punching prevalence; 10–15% annual card loss). **App-first for 50–1,500 students; hardware only for hostel gates/mandated access control.** Some vendors give software away and earn on readers/cards — publishing the honest comparison is a trust differentiator.

## Global AI state of the art (2025–26)

- **PowerSchool PowerBuddy**: ~33 named features — AI rubric scoring with targeted feedback, IEP/504/BIP generators, Socratic-vs-Research tutor modes with teacher-selectable mode per assignment, guardian chat-log review with flagged-content alerts, **"allotments" credit model** (1 credit per generation/regeneration, pooled, no rollover, features pause at zero).
- **Canvas IgniteAI** (Dec 2025): MCP-based agent automating multi-step workflows, **AI Nutrition Facts labels** on every AI feature (models, data accessed, privacy), closed-loop no-training guarantees.
- **Compass MCP**: exposes the SIS to the school's own chosen model ("Your school's data, powering your school's AI. No lock-in").
- **Toddle**: deepest AI in K-12 LMS — curriculum design assistant, assessment builder from objective *or photo of a worksheet*, Feedback & Grading Assistant reading handwriting/PDFs/images, progress reports in 50+ languages; ISO/IEC 42001 + 27701; **all AI gated behind the top tier**.
- **Arbor "Ask Arbor"**: retrieves, surfaces patterns, **takes actions** (send comms, book rooms); Auto Absence turns parent voicemails into pending absence notes; **free monthly AI credit allowance, features pause at zero, no overage billing** (the best buyer-trust model).
- **Free-at-point-of-use AI**: Gemini in Classroom (free, all Workspace editions, 30→50+ features incl. NotebookLM study guides, Gems, standards tagging via CASE); Microsoft Teach + Study agents (free); Khanmigo free for teachers; ChatGPT for Teachers free for US K-12 through June 2028. **Generic lesson-plan generation cannot be sold. Defensible AI sits on proprietary school data, local curriculum/board logic, or workflow execution.**
- **Teacher AI-first tools** (concrete tool lists to emulate): MagicSchool (80+ tools, free tier + $8.33/user/mo), SchoolAI (Spaces, Mission Control, Critical Alert Routing, Notetaker, ESSA Level III validated), Brisk ($99.99/yr, lives where teachers already are), Diffit ($14.99/mo, differentiated passages), Eduaide (~$5.99/mo, 100+ generators), Curipod, Flint (free ≤80 users), Formative Luna (AI governance sold as district feature), CoGrader (handwriting support), EssayGrader, Class Companion, Kahoot (AI behind paywall; **per-feature model disclosure best-in-class**), Wayground/Quizizz ("Zero student data in AI training").
- **Grading evidence**: Toddle claims 90% precision reading handwriting; Gradescope AI-assisted grading is institutional-licence only. **Turnitin AI detection is a credibility problem** (claimed ~98%, disputed independently) — do NOT build detection-as-verdict; build process evidence.
- **AI-in-education backlash is real**: 55% of teachers oppose classroom AI vs 38% support (EdChoice Spring 2026, secondary source — verify); 65% oppose student use; but 72% think students should be taught to use AI well. Teacher workloads: RAND ~49–54 hrs/week. **Gallup (2,232 teachers): weekly AI users save 5.9 hrs/week ≈ six weeks a year**; top tasks: prep 37%, worksheets 33%, modifying materials 28%.
- **The one strong causal result for AI tutoring**: World Bank RCT, Edo State Nigeria — six weeks, twice weekly, teacher-guided ChatGPT with curriculum-aligned prompts → **~0.3 SD ≈ 1.5–2 years of typical progress**, holding up "despite intermittent internet and inconsistent electricity." **The teacher-in-the-loop structure is the finding, not the model.**
- **The cautionary tale**: Nevada's Infinite Campus at-risk model cut the count from 270,000+ to <65,000, defunding poor schools (AI Incident DB #808). **Risk scoring must be advisory, explainable, never wired to money.**
- **PowerSchool breach**: ~62M individuals via one stolen support-portal credential; ransom paid, extortion resumed; Bain Capital kept in MDL (Mar 2026). Infinite Campus breached via Salesforce (ShinyHunters, 137k staff). **Verifiable security is now a sellable feature.**
- **Regulation**: EU AI Act Digital Omnibus (adopted 29 Jun 2026) pushed Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 Dec 2027, but **Article 50 transparency (chatbot disclosure, synthetic-content marking) still bites 2 Aug 2026**; Article 4 AI-literacy duty applies since Feb 2025. Education is an Annex III area — AI grading/admissions scoring is a future high-risk system; architect now. India DPDP: everyone under 18 is a child; verifiable parental consent; **absolute ban on tracking/behavioural monitoring/targeted ads at children regardless of consent**.
- **WhatsApp economics (if used)**: Meta per-message billing since 1 Jul 2025; India utility ~₹0.12–0.13, marketing ~₹0.88; replies in the 24-h window free; no DLT needed (unlike SMS); broadcasts capped at 256 saved contacts.
- **Assessment**: blueprint-aware paper generation is a **solved, commoditised product in India and completely absent in Nepal** — SchoolDeck enforces the CBSE 50% competency/20% MCQ/30% constructed-response split as a hard constraint, auto difficulty ~20/60/20, all 12 CBSE question types, LaTeX maths, Hindi + 7 regional languages, matched OMR, per-exam negative marking, evaluator-style marking keys with source chapter. OMR has collapsed to free phone apps (ZipGrade $6.99/yr; Omrify = on-device AI, no internet, no per-sheet fee). **Do not build an OMR engine; integrate one.** TAO went AGPLv3 open (Dec 2025) shipping **zero AI** — a QTI-compliant AI item generator on top is a credible architecture. **Psychometrics (IRT) lives in desktop tools (jMetrik, Winsteps); no school ERP anywhere embeds item difficulty/discrimination/DIF in the exam module — the most defensible technical differentiator found.**
- **Agent examples in the region**: EduxenOS "Asha" — named AI staff member per tenant: admissions voice callback <2 min (Sarvam STT/TTS + Claude + Exotel), WhatsApp/in-app/SMS concierge, **natural-language record correction that reads the attached proof, drafts the change, routes for approval, rollback-ready with reasoning trace and approver chain**, 22 Indian languages. EdZen AI: **₹7–10 per student per month published** (UPI auto-reconciliation, WhatsApp parent portal, NEP report cards, at-risk monitor).
- **Pricing transparency winners**: Sycamore $4/student/mo all modules + **configurable who-pays on payment fees (3.9%/1.8%/+1%)**; Bromcom fully published G-Cloud per-pupil list; EdZen ₹7–10; Kahoot/Flint/Diffit published. Nepal norm is "public feature list, private number" — **published NPR pricing with a per-student calculator is a wedge.**
